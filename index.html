Halcyon is a concept lander and melt probe for Europa, Jupiter's ice-covered moon. Its job is to cut through about 20 km of ice and reach the ocean underneath.

01 Target

A moon with more water than Earth

Europa is a little smaller than our Moon. Under its frozen crust, scientists think there is a global salty ocean holding about twice the water of all of Earth's oceans combined.

02 Orbit

Squeezed by Jupiter

Europa's orbit is slightly oval, so every 3.55 days it swings close to Jupiter and back out. The changing pull flexes the moon, heats its interior and keeps the ocean liquid.

The rust-coloured streaks across the surface are called lineae. They are cracks where that flexing has split the ice, and some may carry material up from below.

03 Landing

Touchdown on chaos terrain

Halcyon sets down beside a patch of broken, jumbled ice where ocean water may have reached the surface recently. The lander stays on top as a radio relay. The cryobot drops through a hatch in its belly and starts to melt.

04 Ice shell

Twenty kilometres of ice, one heated nose

The cryobot is a heated cylinder about the height of a person. It melts the ice in front of it, lets the meltwater refreeze behind, and unspools a tether to talk to the lander. At around a metre an hour, the trip down takes more than two years.

04 Halfway down

−160 °C at the top, near freezing at the base

Heat leaks upward from the ocean, so the ice warms the deeper the probe goes. Pockets of trapped brine may sit inside the shell. The cryobot samples meltwater as it passes, looking for salts and organic molecules carried up from below.

05 Ocean

Dark, salty, and maybe not empty

No sunlight reaches this water. Anything living here would feed on chemistry from vents in the seafloor, the way life does around hydrothermal vents on Earth.

The glowing life in this scene is imagined. Finding out whether it exists is the point of the mission.

— End of transmission

Signal received 43 minutes later

Light takes roughly 33 to 53 minutes to cross from Jupiter to Earth, depending on where the planets are. Everything the cryobot finds arrives that late.

Halcyon is a design concept, not a real mission, and is not affiliated with NASA or ESA. Figures for Europa are published scientific estimates.
